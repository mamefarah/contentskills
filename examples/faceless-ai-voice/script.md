## Script

Claude can now edit all your videos for free.
Here's exactly how I use it, and the step that makes your videos look good is the one almost everyone skips.
Step one: open Claude Code or ChatGPT Work and type "setup blotato contentskills". It installs everything for you.
Step two: tell it what you want. A topic, a script, or a clip of you talking straight to your phone.
Step three: it plans your whole video, scene by scene, with animations, captions, music and sound effects.
Step four, the one people skip: open the preview, watch it play, and tap Change on any scene you don't like. It fixes it in seconds.
Step five: hit Make my video. It checks that no text covers your face, and if you want something different, just leave a note.
You're the director now, not the editor.
Comment VIDEO and I'll send you the link.

On screen for step one: the words "setup blotato contentskills" typed into the chat box.
Owner's rule for this script: no jargon (no repo, GitHub, npm, terminal). Hook is the owner's.
